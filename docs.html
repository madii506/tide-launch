how TIDE works.

Everything TIDE does, every address it touches, and how to check all of it yourself.

01what it is

TIDE is a launchpad where every coin gets a reserve and a support line. Anyone can launch a coin on the TIDE launchpad on Raydium LaunchLab and pick what its reserve holds: up to four of nine assets that tend to hold up or rise when stocks fall.

1% of every trade of every coin goes to the reserve, which buys each coin's hedge with that coin's share. When a coin falls 30% under its highest price of the last 24 hours, the reserve sells hedge, buys the coin back and burns it. Nobody else takes a cut: no creator fee, no TIDE fee.

02launching a coin

  • Pick a hedge. A ready mix (the classic, crash shield, safe haven, gold bug) or up to four assets of your own, split evenly.
  • Name it. Name, ticker and an image: one of six presets or your own https link.
  • Sign once. One transaction creates the coin on the TIDE launchpad on Raydium LaunchLab: 1,000,000,000 supply, 793.1M on the bonding curve, graduation at 85 SOL into a Raydium CPMM pool. An optional first buy goes in the same transaction.
  • Cost. Solana network fees and rent, about 0.03 SOL, plus any first buy.

04the hedge, on-chain

A coin's hedge is written into its metadata link at launch, so anyone can read it from the chain and nobody can change it. The link looks like this:

/api/tide?a=t&n=Low%20Tide&s=EBB&h=G50U50&i=1

  • h is the hedge: up to four asset codes, each followed by a whole-number weight. Weights add up to 100.
  • Codes: G gold (GLDx), P gold (PAXG), X gold (XAUt0), S silver (SLVon), Q short Nasdaq (SQQQ), V VIX (UVXY), U dollars (USDC), T dollars (USDT), E euros (EURC).
  • n, s, i are the name, the ticker and the image (a preset number, or u plus a base64url https link).

At graduation half of each coin's LP is burned and half is kept as a Raydium fee key, which keeps paying the reserve.

06the reserve

The treasury is the wallet that created the TIDE launchpad. LaunchLab sends the launchpad fee there when it claims. The same wallet reads each coin's hedge from its metadata link and buys it through Jupiter with that coin's share. Every claim and buy is public.

There is no redeem. A coin is not a share of the treasury. Its price is set by its own trading.

06bthe support line

Every coin's support line sits 30% under its highest price of the last 24 hours. While a coin trades under it, the reserve may spend up to a fifth of that coin's share per buyback, at most once an hour: it sells the coin's hedge through Jupiter, buys the coin, and burns what it bought.

In this first version the reserve wallet signs each buyback from the operator console. Every swap and burn is public on Solscan. A reserve slows a dump; it can't stop a big one. There is no floor.

08the API

GET/api/tide?a=basket

the nine hedges, twenty stocks and the S&P 500: price, 24h change, liquidity, volume and the deepest pool.

GET/api/tide?a=coins

every coin on the TIDE launchpad with its hedge, market cap and progress to Raydium.

GET/api/tide?a=t&…

a coin's token metadata, built from its link.

GET/api/tide?a=treasury

what the reserve holds and what's waiting to be claimed.

POST/api/tide?a=buildLaunch

builds the launch transaction for your wallet to sign: wallet, name, symbol, image, hedge, buySol.

09risks

  • A hedge, not a promise. Gold can fall with stocks; the short Nasdaq and VIX bleed in calm weeks.
  • Thin pools. Some hedges have small pools on Solana; big buys move their price.
  • Issuers keep powers. Tokenized funds and stablecoins are issued by third parties that can pause or freeze their tokens.
  • Coins, not shares. A coin can go to zero. Fees only build a hedge if it trades.
