faq

Questions, answered.

What is TIDE?

A launchpad on Raydium LaunchLab where every coin gets a reserve and a support line. 1% of each coin's trading fills its reserve with a hedge. When the coin falls 30% under its 24h high, the reserve buys it back and burns it.

Why hold a hedge instead of SOL?

A hedge tends to be worth the most when everything else is red. Crashes pump the short Nasdaq and VIX, and gold often holds. That's exactly when coins dump.

Is the support line a floor?

No. A reserve slows a dump; it can't stop a big one. A coin can still go to zero.

Who signs the buybacks?

The reserve wallet, from the operator console. In this first version a person presses the button when a coin is under its line. Every buyback and burn is public on Solscan.

What can a reserve hold?

Up to four of nine assets: gold (GLDx, PAXG, XAUt0), silver (SLVon), the short Nasdaq (SQQQ), VIX (UVXY), dollars (USDC, USDT) and euros (EURC). Or a ready mix: balanced, storm, steady or gold.

What does it cost to launch?

Only Solana network fees and rent, about 0.03 SOL. A first buy is optional. TIDE takes nothing.

Can the reserve mix change later?

No. It's written into the coin's metadata link at launch. Launch a new coin for a new mix.

Where do the prices come from?

Dexscreener for live prices, GeckoTerminal for history, and Raydium's launch index for the coins on TIDE.
