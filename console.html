console · for the wallet that runs the TIDE reserve

run the reserve.

Every step is a transaction you sign in your own wallet. The wallet that creates the TIDE launchpad is the reserve: LaunchLab sends the 1% fee from every coin to it when it claims, it buys each coin's hedge, and under a coin's support line it spends that hedge buying the coin back.

01create the TIDE launchpad

One transaction makes the TIDE launchpad on Raydium LaunchLab: 1% of every curve trade to this wallet, no creator fee, and at graduation half the LP burned and half kept as a Raydium fee key. Send the launchpad address over afterwards so the site can open the launcher.

02claim the fees

Moves what every coin on the launchpad has paid from LaunchLab's vault to this wallet.

03fill a coin's reserve

Pick a coin, then spend SOL on its hedge, split the way its creator chose. Each coin's hedge is read from its on-chain metadata link. Swaps go through Jupiter; the wallet keeps 0.05 SOL for fees.

04defend a coin: buy back and burn

Checks the coin against its support line (30% under its 24h high, from GeckoTerminal). Under the line, sell a slice of the reserve's holdings of that coin's hedge straight into the coin through Jupiter, then burn what the reserve bought. The rule: up to a fifth of the coin's share, at most once an hour.
